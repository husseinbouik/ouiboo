# Pre-launch reaction test — landing + pricing (#328)

_A 45-minute moderated test to run before the public launch. Goal: learn
whether first-time visitors understand Ouiboo in seconds and whether the
pricing story lands — before spending on acquisition._

## Setup

- 5-8 participants: 3 travelers who book activities in Morocco, 2 agency
  owners/staff, up to 3 wildcards from the target cities.
- Each session: screen-shared call, landing page (staging) + pricing section,
  participant thinks aloud. Record with consent; one observer takes notes.
- Never explain the product first. The page has to do the talking.

## Script

1. **Five-second test.** Show the hero for 5 seconds, hide it. Ask: what is
   this, who is it for, what would you click?
2. **First click.** Show the page again: "You want to book a day trip near
   Marrakech this weekend — where do you click?" (Repeat for an agency
   owner: "You run tours — where do you click?")
3. **Pricing comprehension.** Scroll to pricing. Ask: what does an agency
   pay, when, and what happens when the trial ends? Watch for surprise at
   auto-renewal — the terms card on the billing page (#324) is the reference.
4. **Trust probe.** "What would make you hesitate to book/pay here?" Note
   every hesitation verbatim.
5. **Waitlist intent.** "Would you join the waitlist? Why / why not?" Then
   watch them actually complete the form (note any friction, including the
   Clarity-consent prompt if it appears).

## Pass thresholds (decide before running)

- >= 80% describe the product correctly after the 5-second test.
- >= 80% first-click the traveler search (travelers) or the agency path
  (agencies) for their task.
- 0 participants surprised by renewal/cancellation terms after reading pricing.
- Every trust hesitation is logged and either fixed or explicitly accepted
  in #251 before launch.
