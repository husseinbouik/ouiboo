# Launch Day Go No Go

Use this on the final launch decision call.

## Go Criteria

- [ ] business bank account verified
- [ ] checkout bank details verified by ops and finance
- [ ] payment rollout decisions signed off
- [ ] admin proof-review SOP approved
- [ ] refund SOP approved
- [ ] payout SOP approved
- [ ] support macros ready
- [ ] support coverage owners assigned
- [ ] staging dress rehearsal completed successfully
- [ ] email delivery confirmed
- [ ] proof upload and admin download confirmed
- [ ] production secrets loaded
- [ ] launch-day dashboard or checklist owner assigned
- [ ] `CMI` hidden in production until approval and live validation complete
- [ ] `CashPlus` hidden in production until approval and live validation complete
- [ ] `Wafacash` not exposed

## Stop Launch Conditions

Do not launch if any of these is true:

- proof uploads are failing
- admin cannot review proofs
- booking confirmations are not updating after proof approval
- email verification is failing
- wallet or payout balances drift from expected outcomes
- the bank transfer instructions shown to travelers are unverified
- support does not know who owns proof review, refunds, or payout exceptions

## Launch-Day Monitoring

Track at least these metrics manually if no dashboard exists:

- new bookings count
- proofs awaiting review
- average proof review time
- failed uploads
- refund requests
- payout requests
