# Agency Demo Script — closed beta (#633)

**Use only when recruitment is authorised** (legal gates closed — see
`AGENCY_ONBOARDING_RUNBOOK.md`). One demo ≈ 25 minutes. The script shows the
product as it is; it promises nothing the product or the draft agreement
does not already say.

## Before the demo (operator, 5 minutes)

- **Warm the surfaces first.** The demo runs on free-tier hosting and the
  first hit after an idle period can be slow (~13 s cold start — decision
  record #441). Open every surface you will show, once, before the call or
  before sending any demo link, so the agency's first click is not the cold
  one. Never send a demo link "cold" and never apologise for a wait you
  could have pre-warmed away.
- Use the showcase/staging surfaces with example content clearly labelled
  as example. Do not demo with another agency's real data.
- Have the objection sheet (`AGENCY_OBJECTION_FAQ.md`) open. If a question
  is not covered there, the answer is "I will confirm in writing" — never
  an invented term.

## The script

**1. What Ouiboo is (2 min).** "Ouiboo is a marketplace where licensed
Moroccan agencies publish their trips and travellers book them. You are the
seller: the trip contract is between you and the traveller, travellers pay
**you** directly, and Ouiboo provides the technology and promotion. Ouiboo
never holds traveller money."

**2. The beta offer (3 min).** "We are opening a small closed beta: 3–5
verified agencies in Tangier–Tétouan–Chefchaouen, invited travellers only.
It is **completely free** — no platform service fee, no subscription, no
credits. In exchange we ask for your documents up front (licence, insurance,
bank attestation) and your feedback. If a paid phase ever follows, it would
need a **separate signed agreement** — there is no price and no date for
one, and nothing converts automatically."

**3. Traveller side (5 min).** Show: a trip page (seller-identity block —
your legal name, licence number and date, issuing authority, insurance
status), checkout with staged payment (deposit first, balance after the
group is confirmed), the booking confirmation, and the trip-contacts card
(your emergency contact first, Ouiboo escalation second). Point at the
verification wording: **"Checked before listing"** — what was checked is
exactly what the B18 pack records, no more.

**4. Agency side (8 min).** Show: creating a trip and sessions, the
verification section (document upload + statuses), bank-details submission
(details reach travellers only after the attestation check), a booking
arriving, payment-proof review and bank confirmation ("a proof is not
settlement — your bank confirmation is"), and the refund-case view (refund
due date, evidence upload). Show the assisted-listing offer for the first
trips: "we prepare the first listings with you; nothing publishes until you
approve the exact listing."

**5. The rules that protect both sides (4 min).** Licence or insurance
expiry pauses listings and new bookings automatically — no grace period, so
renewals go in early. Refunds: when one is due, you initiate it within 7
days and complete it against bank evidence on the platform; overdue refunds
pause new bookings until completed. Support split: platform and booking
questions are Ouiboo's lane (reply within 48 hours); trip-logistics
questions are yours; on trip day your emergency contact is the traveller's
first call.

**6. Close (3 min).** "Next step is documents, not commitment: I send you
one checklist (the B18 pack). When the pack is complete and signed off, we
sign the beta agreement and set up your first listings together." Agree the
date for the document ask before ending the call, and record the demo in
the tracker the same day.

## After the demo

- Tracker → `DEMO DONE` with the date; note objections raised (feed new
  ones back into the FAQ sheet — with truthful answers only).
- Send the B18 checklist as the single follow-up. No pricing sheet exists
  for the beta; do not create one.
