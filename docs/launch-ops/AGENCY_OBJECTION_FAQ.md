# Agency Objection / FAQ Sheet — closed beta (#633)

Answers are the **truthful** ones, matched to the draft beta agreement and
the product as built. If an answer is not on this sheet, the answer is "I
will confirm in writing" — never an invented price, date, or term.

**"What does it cost?"**
Nothing during the closed beta: no platform service fee, no subscription,
no credits. That is written into the beta agreement.

**"What will it cost after the beta?"**
No price exists yet, and no date for a paid phase exists. **Any paid phase
would need a separate signed agreement** — you would see the full terms,
including any platform service fee (charged to the agency, invoiced
monthly, never deducted from traveller payments), before agreeing to
anything. Nothing converts automatically when the beta ends; if you sign
nothing, nothing is charged.

**"Who sells the trip — you or us?"**
You do. Every trip contract is between your agency and the traveller.
Ouiboo provides technology and promotion only; it is not the seller or the
organiser, and it says so on the trip page and at checkout, where your
seller-identity block (legal name, licence, insurance status) is shown.

**"How do we get paid?"**
Travellers pay **your agency directly** by bank transfer to your own
account. Ouiboo never receives or holds traveller money. The traveller
uploads a payment proof; the booking settles when **you** confirm the money
arrived in your bank — a proof alone is not settlement.

**"Why do you need our licence, insurance, and bank attestation?"**
Because your seller block and your payment details are shown to travellers
as checked. The licence (ODV) and RC insurance are sighted and recorded,
and bank details reach travellers only after the bank attestation in your
legal name has been sighted. The public wording is "Checked before
listing" — that check is what stands behind it. No verification timeframe
is promised; what is promised is that nothing lists before the check
passes.

**"What happens when our licence or insurance expires?"**
Listings and new bookings pause automatically — there is no grace period —
and restart when the renewed document is sighted and recorded. Bookings
already confirmed are handled under the agreement. Send renewals early.

**"Who sets prices and cancellation terms?"**
You set your prices. Cancellation terms are displayed per trip; the
platform carries a refund floor (for example, a full refund of amounts paid
when a trip's minimum group is not reached or when you cancel), adopted as
part of your listing terms in the agreement — counsel confirms the final
wording before signature.

**"What do we have to do when a refund is due?"**
You pay the traveller directly. You initiate the refund within 7 days of
the due date recorded on the platform and complete it against bank
evidence recorded on the case. While a refund is overdue, new bookings
pause until it is completed on evidence.

**"Do we have to be exclusive to Ouiboo?"**
The draft beta agreement contains no exclusivity clause. (Final text is
counsel's; if that changes, it will be in the agreement you are asked to
sign, not in a sales conversation.)

**"What support do we have to provide?"**
Trip-logistics questions and messages about your trips are your lane.
During your trips' active dates, your named emergency contact is the
traveller's first call — that is why the contact is collected at
onboarding, with the Law 09-08 notice given to that person. Platform and
booking questions are Ouiboo's lane; Ouiboo is the traveller's escalation
during active trips if you cannot be reached.

**"What happens to our data / our travellers' data?"**
Booking data reaches you solely to run the trip. The onboarding pack
includes the Law 09-08 Art. 5 notices for your legal representative and
your emergency contact, whose details are processed on the platform.
Travellers are never asked to send identity documents through the platform.

**"How many travellers will we get?"**
No number is promised. The beta is capped and invitation-only by design —
its purpose is to prove the flow end-to-end with a small cohort, not to
deliver volume. Any agency quoting you a guaranteed volume for a closed
beta is inventing it; we do not.

**"Can we leave?"**
Either side can end beta participation on the notice the agreement states;
bookings already confirmed are then performed or refunded under the
agreement's refund rules. There is no paid plan to cancel — the beta is
free.
