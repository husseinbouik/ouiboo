# Product Vision

## Ouiboo

Ouiboo is an **agency-first travel platform and traveler marketplace**.

Its purpose is to connect travelers with curated trips offered by verified travel agencies while giving agencies the essential digital tools required to publish, sell and manage those trips.

Ouiboo is not trying to become Booking.com, Salesforce, SAP, Expedia and QuickBooks simultaneously. Its strength should come from connecting traveler demand with verified local travel agencies and progressively giving those agencies useful travel-specific operating tools.

## Value for travelers

Ouiboo should help a traveler:

- discover curated trips;
- understand which agency sells and operates a trip;
- see whether that agency is verified;
- understand price, departure availability and relevant policies;
- book through the supported workflow;
- complete the supported payment process;
- see confirmation and booking status;
- know the basic support/cancellation path.

The underlying positioning principle is: help travelers discover and book curated trips from verified local travel agencies with transparent information and direct local expertise. This is a product principle, not immutable marketing copy and not a guarantee of insurance, payment protection or unconditional refunds.

## Value for agencies

Ouiboo should help an agency:

- register and provide its business information;
- become verified;
- create trips and publish real departures;
- expose bookable inventory online;
- receive and manage bookings;
- review payment confirmation in the supported MVP workflow;
- build traveler trust through a clear public identity;
- progressively reduce fragmented travel-specific operations.

Some agencies may currently combine WhatsApp, Instagram, spreadsheets, email, PDFs, phone calls and manual transfer tracking. Ouiboo's ability to improve that workflow is a **product hypothesis** until it is validated with real agencies. It must not be documented as an established market fact.

## Marketplace strategy

Ouiboo is **agency-first, but not agency-only**:

```text
useful agency tools
→ agencies publish real inventory
→ the marketplace becomes useful to travelers
→ travelers generate bookings
→ the marketplace becomes more valuable to agencies
```

The immediate operating principle is:

> FIX → STABILIZE → VALIDATE → PILOT → LEARN

Repeated problems observed with real users—not competitor checklists—should determine later priorities.

## Product validation milestone

The next major milestone is:

> Real agencies can publish real trips and manage real bookings, while real travelers can discover and book those trips without the Ouiboo developer manually guiding them.

A controlled pilot should recruit a small number of agencies, help only where necessary, observe onboarding failures, publish real inventory, test with real travelers, collect structured feedback and measure the funnel. A single feature request is evidence to investigate, not an automatic implementation commitment.
